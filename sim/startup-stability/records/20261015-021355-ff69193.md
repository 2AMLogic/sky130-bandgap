- **Claim**: spec/topology-survey.md Startup row ("self-starting, < 1 ms") -- the no-other-stable-state half. Substantiates that design/bandgap_core.sch + design/startup_injector.sch has exactly ONE DC operating point over the whole 0..VDD range of its startup node GDRV, that the zero-current state the bare core does have (reproduced here as the control instance) is actively driven away from by microamps rather than merely being unlikely, and that the loop is pulled downhill by a bounded-below current everywhere in the degenerate region rather than merely somewhere in it.
