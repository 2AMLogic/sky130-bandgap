## Sibling check

The GF180 DUT used for the re-null cited by DR-009 has a 5 × 5 µm Q1 and a
10 × 10 µm Q2. In its pinned gf180mcuD library (open_pdks `c6d73a35`,
`gf180mcu_fd_pr` at `faef89e8`), both cards set the forward emission
coefficient `nf = 1`. The sky130 pair's differential emission-coefficient
term is therefore absent from that GF180 comparison. Other model parameters
still differ. The newer GF180 schematic uses one and four copies of the same
5 × 5 µm device, a directly applicable precedent for the unit-array topology.

## Candidate sizing

- **`unit-pair`.** Keep Q1's eight small units. At 27 °C the identical-unit
  approximation gives ΔVBE ≈ 1.028 × 25.86 mV × ln(N). Targeting the
  measured 60.52 mV gives N ≈ 9.74. Choose 1:10, or 8:80 units, with the
  stock R2/R1. The 88 emitters occupy 40.69 µm² of active emitter area.
  Series resistance and finite beta make the full core sweep necessary.
  [PNP ladder](../../pnp-characterization/records/20260801-041501-48ac24d.md).
- **`mixed-r1`.** Keep three of seven 5 µm high-poly units and replace the
  other four (4/7 of 14,026.89 Ω ≈ 8,015 Ω at 27 °C) with one 1 × 3.77 µm
  xhigh-poly resistor. The measured 1 × 5 µm xhigh unit is 10,627.5 Ω at
  27 °C. This approximately preserves nominal R1 and increases R2/R1
  toward the hot end because the simulated xhigh resistance is nearly flat
  while high poly rises. The single-length experiment found a discrepancy
  with the xhigh model card's −1,470 ppm/°C coefficient. Any simulated pass
  for this variant would need an independent resistor-TC check before design-in.
  [R1 chain](../../res-array-head-resistance/records/20260805-113409-6caa9f8.md),
  [poly sweep](../../resistor-flavor-characterization/records/20260731-044337-a8c4147.md),
  [single-length experiment](../../resistor-tc-single-length/records/20260731-073440-3dfe830.md).
- **`hot-pnp`.** Connect the emitter of one small PNP to VOUT through
  three 5 µm high-poly units (about 6.01 kΩ). Connect its base to VA and
  its substrate collector to VSS. As VA falls with temperature, the rising
  VOUT−VA forward-biases this PNP. Its base current adds to Q1's emitter
  current and increases ΔVBE. The series chain limits the hot current.
  The bench charges the entire added emitter current to Iq through V1.
  [PNP ladder](../../pnp-characterization/records/20260801-041501-48ac24d.md),
  [unit-chain resistance](../../res-array-head-resistance/records/20260805-113409-6caa9f8.md).

## Batch measurements

Run each of the following on a worker with the sim-submit profile and the
artifact broker. Each run writes a separate append-only candidate record.

```sh
sim/tc-curvature-candidates/run_candidates.py unit-pair --backend batch
sim/tc-curvature-candidates/run_candidates.py mixed-r1 --backend batch
sim/tc-curvature-candidates/run_candidates.py hot-pnp --backend batch
```

Each run covers 5 processes × 3 outer temperatures × 3 supplies. Each outer
corner repeats the stock 16-point −40…125 °C sweep. Read box TC (<50 ppm/°C),
VREF minimum, 27 °C and maximum (all within 1.176–1.224 V), and worst Iq
(<50 µA) from its record. The per-corner Iq change compares the candidate's
current at the outer temperature with the archived stock operating point.
The 27 °C current uses the same interpolation as the 27 °C VREF reading.

For candidates clearing those rows and the area screen, repeat the command
with `--bench psrr-dc` and `--bench startup-stability`. The former checks
the weakest rejection at `sf / −40 °C / 2.97 V`. The latter checks the
smallest traversal current at `ss / 125 °C / 2.97 V` and the largest
injector standing current at `ff / 125 °C / 3.63 V`.
[PSRR baseline](../../psrr-dc/records/20261112-073424-f9871d93.md),
[startup baseline](../../startup-stability/records/20261111-234331-ebc9a64b.md).
